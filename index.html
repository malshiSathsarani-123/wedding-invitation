The Day, Beautifully Planned

Sacred traditions & celebrations
09:15 AM Welcome

Guests Arrival & Welcome

Warm reception with chilled King Coconut water, herbal refreshments, and traditional treats.

09:48 AM Nakatha

Auspicious Poruwa rites, lighting the traditional oil lamp, and Jayamangala Gatha blessings.

Registration & Ring Exchange

Signing of the marriage registry, exchanging vows, and sealing our promises with wedding rings.
